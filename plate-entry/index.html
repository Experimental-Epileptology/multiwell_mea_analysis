Plate details

Time accepts hours or a text label such as baseline or day 3. Plate IDs identify physical plates and must be unique across experiments.

Custom fields

Keep the standard fields and add your own. Shared fields apply to every well in an append; per-well fields get a plate grid. All custom fields are optional.

Enter conditions in plate view

Recording names, Batch, DIV and dates can differ by well. Empty recording-name overrides use the shared default; names are cleared after each append. Dates accept compact, separated or named-month formats (e.g. 030425, 03.04.2025, 3 April 2025) and are saved as YYYY-MM-DD. Ambiguous dates are day-first; two-digit years mean 2000–2099; DIV is entered manually as days in vitro. Choose a field, then type into wells or paste a rectangular block from Excel. Paste starts at the focused well. Only wells with a cell line are included. Exclude marks a well for filtering; its record is still appended. Use Notes for the reason.

How timepoints work

Leave a well’s time or dose unit empty to use the shared value above. A number or text label overrides the shared time. Dose may be left empty. For repeated measurements, keep the plate and conditions, change the shared time, and append again. For destructive sampling, assign each collection to a different physical well or plate. The example demonstrates entry, not an experimental allocation recommendation.
